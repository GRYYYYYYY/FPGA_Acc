# Cost boundaries

The primary measurement is the supervisor's `time.monotonic_ns()` interval from immediately before process creation until process termination. Both arms use the same isolated source, Python, CPU31 affinity, one VPR worker and thread environment. No cache flushing is performed. Each arm receives one warmup per exposed development case. Shared-host load observations are retained per process; these are serial warm-cache diagnostics, not dedicated-machine confidence intervals.

Refinement-only begins with the frozen legal placement/routing, packed netlist, RR graph and endpoint/criticality contract already present. It includes interpreter/import startup, source/input hashing, loading, initial feedback extraction, proposal construction and scoring, accepted AND rejected native closures, feedback refresh, rollback, final placement validation, output writing, and terminal report serialization. The initial contract validation and final source/input verification are inside the measured process. No necessary native validation is removed from A.

The timing tree's exclusive root children are preparation, proposal, oracle_driver, feedback_refresh, search_other, output, and external_unattributed. Their sum is checked against the same run's outer time. Native VPR wall time is a child of oracle_driver. Index construction/replacement and cache invalidation timers are diagnostic children spanning existing preparation/search phases; they must not be added to the root. Driver acceptance/rejection costs are matched to individual trials, not inferred from acceptance percentages. No median of stages is added to create a fictitious median run.

Scientific signature comparisons after process exit are offline audit costs. They are separately reported and check the report/candidate hashes, trial decisions and numeric feedback identities; they do not replace the online acceptance checks. Full enumeration of all rejected internal proposal alternatives is not serialized by the existing implementation: exact comparison covers all candidates submitted to native closure and their transaction costs, plus phase decisions/counts. That observability limit is retained.

Cold deployment begins at a common already packed, legally placed/routed native design. TP additionally needs connection export, graph/connection packing, PR-derived input paths, proxy criticality, timing conversion and contract generation. These are not free and are not required of B1. The historical cohort stage receipts are preserved, including failed path preparation. A historical single refinement process plus its measured method-specific preparation stages is an **accounted composite**, not a newly observed outer cold-deployment interval. Cross-date composite times must not be interpreted as new A/B cold speedups.

`historical_cohort_aggregate_s` contains preparation, two refinement repeats, and driver overhead. It is not divided by two. Independent repeat process receipts provide genuine per-repeat refinement times. The cohort preparation stage called `baseline` includes common initial native work and RR-graph export: its internal split is unknown. Excluding that entire stage is only valid for the explicitly frozen prepared-input boundary, not for an all-inclusive deployment claim.

This round does not rerun the PR-derived preparation pipeline. The new cold-deployment total therefore remains unknown. Original VPR B0 is only the common starting point. B1 includes reading its inputs, fresh SA initialization, placement, complete routing/analysis, output validation, hashes and writes. Common netlist/architecture preparation is excluded symmetrically; TP-specific preprocessing cannot be excluded from a later cold comparison.

Same wall time and same native-closure count answer different questions. SA moves are not TP closures. Future equal-wall analysis may select only legal, fully validated outputs completed before the cutoff; an over-budget candidate cannot become a budget-compliant result. This pilot makes no equal-budget superiority claim.

Instrumentation is included in all measured process times. The raw `index_build_s` diagnostic measures the pressure snapshot/index setter; B includes a nested cache clear whereas A records its later clear separately. These small diagnostic timers are not used as mutually exclusive A/B totals. Counters, the switch branch and edgewalk helper call are not subtracted or claimed to represent an uninstrumented production build.
