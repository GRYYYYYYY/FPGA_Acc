\section{Artifact and Environment}

The frozen artifact targets Ubuntu 22.04, Linux 5.15.0-190, VTR 9.0.0,
Vitis/Vivado/Vitis HLS 2022.2, XRT 2.14.354, and the U280 2022.2 development
platform. No physical U280
is attached to the evaluation VM. The machine-readable paper manifest is
\texttt{paper/generated/}\allowbreak\texttt{paper\_results.json};
\texttt{SHA256SUMS} authenticates
the derived CSV, JSON, LaTeX, and SVG files. The generator fails if a CPU pair
does not pass or differs in route hash, wirelength, CPD, or routing iterations.

\section{Frozen Inputs and Commands}

The matrix contains VTR \texttt{diffeq}, \texttt{frisc}, \texttt{elliptic}, and
\texttt{clma}; Titan23 \texttt{neuron} and \texttt{stereo\_vision}; and Koios
2.0 \texttt{conv\_layer}, \texttt{attention\_layer}, \texttt{spmv}, and
\texttt{softmax}. VTR cases use seed 1/channel width 200. Titan23 uses seed
3/channel width 300. Koios uses seed 1/channel width 300 and the official
hard-memory include. Each serial/parallel pair uses the same TBB VPR binary and
changes only \texttt{--num\_workers} from 1 to 16.

\begin{quote}\ttfamily\small
python3 tools/prepare\_paper\_artifacts.py\par
(cd paper/generated \&\&\par
\quad sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS)\par
ctest --test-dir BUILD --output-on-failure\par
python3 tools/test\_converters.py\par
python3 tools/write\_vpr\_route.py \ldots\par
python3 tools/validate\_vpr\_route.py \ldots\par
python3 ../experiments/strong\_baselines/\par
\quad test\_runner.py
\end{quote}

The Xilinx flow must source the Vitis 2022.2 and XRT setup scripts. Long
synthesis/implementation jobs are serialized on the 16-GiB host. The complete
hardware XCLBIN uses four \texttt{search\_hbm\_kernel} CUs, one
\texttt{commit\_hbm\_kernel} CU, and 32 HBM pseudo-channels at a requested
180\,MHz data clock.

\section{Validation and Claim Boundary}

Seven CTest targets, converter tests, HLS C simulation, XSIM co-simulation,
full U280 implementation, two real-VPR-subset hardware-emulation tests, and 15
VPR/Tatum timing-oracle iterations pass. The commercial bridge is checked by
FPGA Interchange round-trip, the official FPGA24 checker, and Vivado routing
legality/DRC. Exported \texttt{frisc} and \texttt{elliptic} routes also pass
VPR-native routing consistency and feasibility checks; their CPD deltas are
+0.884\%/+0.549\%, while wirelength deltas are +3.656\%/+3.864\%.
Hardware-emulation time is excluded from performance claims.
Submission-quality speedup and energy require a physical U280, at least five
post-warm-up repetitions, end-to-end phase timing, power integration, and the
ablations listed in the artifact's \texttt{EXPERIMENT\_PLAN.md}.
